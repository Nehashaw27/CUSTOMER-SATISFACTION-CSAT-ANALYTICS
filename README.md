CUSTOMER SATISFACTION (CSAT) ANALYTICS
Project Overview
The objective of this project is to analyze customer satisfaction data and identify patterns in customer calls, communication channels, customer locations, call reasons, and response times.
Power BI was used to visualize the data and understand customer service performance.
Customer Satisfaction Overview
This dashboard presents an overview of customer service calls and customer satisfaction metrics. It includes KPI cards and visualizations to analyze call trends, communication channels, states, reasons for calling, and response times.
 

1. Executive Summary & Purpose
The Customer Satisfaction Analysis (CSAT) Dashboard provides an executive overview of customer support operations, call volumes, Service Level Agreement (SLA) compliance, and customer satisfaction ratings. It enables service managers and operational teams to monitor performance trends, identify primary inquiry drivers, and evaluate channel effectiveness.

3. Dashboard Visual Breakdown & Insights
A. Daily Customer Call Trends (Line Chart)
•	Fields Used: Count of id by call_timestamp (October 2020 timeframe).
•	Purpose: Tracks day-over-day call volume fluctuations to support workforce scheduling and capacity planning.
•	Key Observation: Daily incoming call volume remains steady just above the 1K mark across the displayed dates.
 
B. Customer Calls by Response Time (Horizontal Bar Chart)
•	Fields Used: Total Calls categorized by response_time status.
•	Breakdown: Within SLA (~21K calls), Below SLA (~8K calls), and Above SLA (~4K calls).
•	Key Observation: While the majority of calls meet SLA targets, roughly one-third (~12K calls combined) fall outside optimal response thresholds.

 

C. Customer Calls by Reason (Vertical Column Chart)
•	Fields Used: Total Calls grouped by inquiry reason.
•	Breakdown: Billing Question (23K), Payments (5K), and Service Outage (5K).
•	Key Observation: Billing Questions account for ~70% of all customer inquiries, making billing clarity the single biggest opportunity to reduce call volume.
 

D. Customer Contact Channels (Donut Chart)
•	Fields Used: Total Calls split by support channel (Total: 32.941K).
•	Breakdown: Call-Center (10.64K / 32.3%), Chatbot (8.26K / 25.06%), Email (7.47K / 22.68%), and Web (6.58K / 19.96%).
•	Key Observation: Customer engagement is evenly balanced between direct human support (Call-Center & Email at ~55%) and digital self-service channels (Chatbot & Web at ~45%).
 

E. Customer Calls by State (Bubble Map)
•	Fields Used: Total Calls plotted geographically by state across the United States.
•	Key Observation: Call volume density is highest across the East Coast, Midwest, Texas, and California, correlating with major population hubs.
 


4. Business Insights
Based on the CSAT dashboard analysis, the following business insights were identified:
1.	High Call Volume: A total of approximately 32,941 customer interactions were recorded, indicating significant customer support activity.
2.	Customer Satisfaction: The average CSAT score is 2.07, indicating an opportunity to improve customer satisfaction.
3.	SLA Performance: Approximately 21,000 calls were handled within SLA, representing around 63.75% of total calls. The remaining calls require attention to improve response performance.
4.	Billing-Related Issues: Billing questions account for approximately 23,000 calls, making billing-related queries the largest source of customer support demand.
5.	Communication Channels: The Call-Center channel contributes approximately 32.3% of total calls, followed by Chatbot, Email and Web.
6.	Geographical Distribution: The map indicates higher customer call activity across several major US regions, including the East Coast, Midwest, Texas and California.
7.	Digital Support Opportunities: Chatbot and Web channels together contribute approximately 45% of customer interactions, showing the importance of digital customer support


5. Business Recommendations
Based on the dashboard findings, the following recommendations are suggested:
1.	Improve Billing Transparency: Simplify billing statements and provide clear explanations of charges to reduce repeated billing-related inquiries.
2.	Improve SLA Performance: Identify the reasons for delayed responses and allocate additional support agents during peak periods.
3.	Enhance Chatbot Services: Improve chatbot capabilities to resolve common customer queries without requiring human intervention.
4.	Improve Customer Satisfaction: Conduct regular customer feedback surveys and identify the reasons behind low CSAT scores.
5.	Optimize Workforce Planning: Use daily call trends to schedule customer service representatives according to customer demand.
6.	Monitor Regional Performance: Analyze state-wise customer service demand and ensure sufficient support resources in high-volume regions.
7.	Continuous Performance Monitoring: Track CSAT scores, call volumes and SLA compliance regularly to measure service improvements.

6. Project conclusion
The Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) Analytics project provides valuable insights into customer service operations using Power BI.
The analysis covers customer call trends, communication channels, geographical distribution, reasons for customer inquiries, response times and customer satisfaction.
The findings highlight billing questions as a major driver of customer support interactions and identify opportunities to improve SLA compliance and customer satisfaction.
By implementing the recommended improvements, the company can optimize customer support operations, reduce response delays and enhance the overall customer experience.
